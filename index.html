Exam Structure · 100% Class 12 NCERT

CUET UG 2027 Pattern

Syllabus Scope: Strictly Class 12 NCERT syllabus (Class 11 is excluded).

Paper Format: 50 compulsory MCQs per subject · 60 minutes per paper · +5 correct / −1 incorrect (250 marks per paper).

Your 5-Paper Combination (1250 Marks): English (Section IA) + Mathematics + Physics + Chemistry (Domain Section II) + General Aptitude Test / GAT (Section III).

CUET Strategy: Speed and NCERT line-by-line precision matter most (1.2 minutes per question). Direct NCERT examples, tables, named reactions, graphs, and statement-based questions dominate.
Class 12-Only Focus · Excluding Class 11

JEE Main 2027 Pattern (Class 12 Share)

Paper Format: 25 questions per subject (20 MCQs + 5 compulsory Numericals) · +4 correct / −1 incorrect (300 marks total).

Class 12 Weightage Share: Class 12 chapters contribute 52%–55% of the entire JEE Main paper (~13–14 questions per subject, or ~156–168 marks out of 300).

Execution Order: Your daily roadmap prioritizes Tier 1 High-Yield Class 12 chapters first so you lock in maximum marks for both JEE Main (Jan) and Boards/CUET.

Post-Board Transition: From 1 March, shift to pure CUET PCM speed drills; from 1 April, daily English + GAT blocks automatically appear in your Daily Plan.
