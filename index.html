CUET UG pattern (Class 12 NCERT)

Syllabus: Strictly Class 12 NCERT (Class 11 excluded).

Format: 50 MCQs per paper · 60 mins · +5 / −1 (250 marks each).

Subjects: Maths, Physics, Chemistry + English (from 1 March) + GAT (from 1 April).

Focus on NCERT lines, tables, graphs, exceptions, and timed speed (72 sec/question).

JEE Main pattern (Class 12 share)

Format: 25 questions per subject (20 MCQs + 5 numericals) · +4 / −1.

Class-12 Share: ~52–55% of paper (~13–14 Qs per subject, Class 11 excluded).

Order: High-yield Class-12 chapters lead the sprint so JEE, Boards, and CUET stay aligned.

Physics & Chemistry chapter durations include +2 extra days per chapter; Maths keeps its exact day count.
